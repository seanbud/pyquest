# Course attribution and licensing

Pyquest was created around the exercises in Alex's
[Python: Zero to Hero](https://github.com/Alendro305/python-learning) repository.
Alex's project is the source of the lesson instructions, starter files, tests,
and demonstrations used by the bundled course manifest.

Those course materials are **not included in the Pyquest repository** and are
not covered by Pyquest's MIT license. At the time of this release, the upstream
repository does not declare a license. The public browser app retrieves the
materials from their original GitHub location; local users provide their own
checkout. All rights in those materials remain with their respective author.

The MIT license in this repository applies only to the Pyquest interface,
runtime, course-manifest format, and project documentation authored here.
